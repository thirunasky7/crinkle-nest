Bakehouse FAQs

Do you deliver warm across Bangalore? expand_more
Yes! We arrange temperature-controlled doorstep delivery for celebration cakes and morning breakfast bakes within a 25km radius from our Heritage Quarter hearth.
How much notice is needed for tiered cakes? expand_more
We recommend 48 to 72 hours for bespoke multi-tiered floral cakes so our bakers can ferment sponges slowly and source edible garden blooms fresh.
Can you craft 100% eggless or vegan bakes? expand_more
Over 75% of our menu—including our signature chocolate crinkles, salted caramel tea cakes, and sourdoughs—is crafted 100% eggless with organic churned dairy or oat milk.
