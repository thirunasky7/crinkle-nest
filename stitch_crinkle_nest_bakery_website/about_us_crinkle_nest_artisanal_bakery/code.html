From Our Hands to Your Table

“A homemade bake carries a piece of the baker’s heart. We fold warmth into every single tin.”

What began in winter 2021 as a weekend therapeutic baking ritual in Tara’s tiny apartment kitchen soon took over every tabletop. Madeleine brought classical French lamination precision, while Tara perfected the slow-fermentation crinkle technique that defines our signatures today.

We reject the industrial shortcuts common to modern confectionery. There are no premixes, no stabilizers to make our cookies sit on store shelves for months, and no compromise on single-estate butter. When an order lands at our hearth, it is prepared specifically for your tea time.
