## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory comfort, tactile warmth, and refined craftsmanship of an artisanal bakery boutique. The aesthetic marries French patisserie sophistication with the warm, nostalgic atmosphere of an intimate hearth kitchen.

### Core Character & Atmosphere
- **Sensory Indulgence:** Surfaces evoke baked pastry crusts, whipped cream, dusted cinnamon, and molten chocolate. Imagery and UI elements emphasize texture, crumb structure, and honeyed glaze.
- **Warm Editorial Minimalist:** Abundant creamy negative space, organic pillowy silhouettes, and curated typography ensure an editorial, Pinterest-native composition rather than a cluttered commercial storefront.
- **Approachable Luxury:** Tactile, rounded surfaces and gentle press states project approachable authenticity, framing custom cakes, sourdoughs, and flaky pastries as affordable culinary art.
- **Primary Emotional Tone:** Warm, comforting, appetizing, intimate, and effortlessly refined.

## Colors

The color palette centers on culinary gastronomy: deep roasted cocoa, oven-caramelized sugars, folded flour creams, and soft strawberry glaze.

### Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#3D2314` - Dark Chocolate):** Used for primary typography, authoritative headings, luxury outline accents, and high-emphasis callouts. Anchors the soft backdrop with readable, rich contrast.
- **Secondary (`#C87D38` - Caramel Gold):** Dedicated to conversion drivers, badges, active hover glows, price highlights, and primary ordering prompts.
- **Tertiary (`#F7D6D0` - Pastel Blush Pink):** Acts as a soft accent for seasonal tags, dietary markers (e.g., GF, vegan), decorative sticker chips, and tender pill highlights.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Cream (`#FFFDF9` to `#FDF8F0`).
  - Card & Container Fill: Biscuit Beige (`#F5EDE0` to `#EAD8C0`).
  - Deep Text & Accents: Bitter Chocolate (`#2A170C`).
- **Functional States:**
  - Order Messaging / WhatsApp Accent: Deep warm olive green (`#3F5E3D`) or tinted forest gold, keeping social actions harmonious without breaking the pastry palette.
  - Error: Baked Raspberry (`#A83232`).
  - Success: Sweet Pistachio (`#4E7246`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-contrast, editorial serif headings (`Playfair Display`) with an approachable, open, geometric body face (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

- **Display & Headings:** Set with fluid line heights and subtle negative tracking to mimic gourmet menu prints and high-end culinary publications. Editorial titles feature occasional italic accents for seasonal pastry specials or origins (e.g., *“Single-Origin Madagascar Vanilla”*).
- **Body & Captions:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides legibility on mobile screens, retaining rounded junctions that echo the soft pillowy curvature of the layout components.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Formatted in slightly letterspaced medium and semibold weights for badge tags, batch countdowns, and dietary indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an organic, fluid 12-column grid on desktop and a versatile 4-column system on mobile, prioritizing breathable margins and visual rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem), `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Single-column stack for food story modules, 2-column masonry grid for pastry gallery items.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Product cards group cleanly in 2-column or 3-column configurations.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns max container width of `1280px`, `margin` (3rem), `gutter` (1.5rem).

### Layout Rules
- Allow expansive vertical spacing between curated pastry collections (`space-xl`).
- Use asymmetrical offsets and staggered vertical alignments on gallery imagery to emulate an artfully composed tabletop spread.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold greys and hard cast shadows. Instead, it relies on warm, honey-tinted ambient glows and layered biscuit-cream surfaces.

### Amber-Tinted Ambient Shadows
- **Resting Surface (Cards, Nav Pills):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(200, 125, 56, 0.04)`. Soft and pillowed.
- **Elevated Hover (Interactive Cards, Sticky Basket):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(200, 125, 56, 0.08)`. Creates a light, floating brioche-like lift.
- **Floating Modals & WhatsApp Action Trays:** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(42, 23, 12, 0.16)`. Deep roasted contrast that ensures legibility.

### Surface Tiers & Overlays
- **Tonal Layers:** Level 0 (Canvas `#FFFDF9`) → Level 1 (Product Surface `#FDF8F0`) → Level 2 (Inset Wells / Filter Containers `#F5EDE0`).
- **Glass Accents:** Translucent frosted overlays use `background: rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a whisper-thin biscuit border (`rgba(200, 125, 56, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pillowy, organic language (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Cards & Visual Containers:** Sculpted with generous `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem) and `rounded-3xl` (2rem) radii, reflecting the plump, baked curves of pastries and brioches.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Strictly full-pill (`9999px`) rounded geometry, delivering a tactile, finger-friendly feel.
- **Framing & Media:** Food photography cards use smooth inner masks with soft corners; hero images occasionally incorporate gentle archway masks (half-circle tops with rounded bases) to reference traditional hearth ovens.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA ("Order via WhatsApp", "Reserve Batch"):** Pill-shaped, rich chocolate (`#3D2314`) or warm caramel (`#C87D38`) fill with cream text (`#FFFDF9`). Includes an organic micro-bounce hover effect (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) and a subtle golden amber shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Soft biscuit fill (`#F5EDE0`) with chocolate text and a delicate border (`#EAD8C0`). Smooth active state transitioning into blush pink (`#FCEAE6`).
- **WhatsApp Action Trigger:** Soft caramel gold or warm moss base featuring an embedded WhatsApp badge icon, dynamic product-count pill, and explicit pricing prompt.

### Pastry Cards & Product Displays
- **Container:** Layered cream-to-biscuit background (`#FDF8F0`), `rounded-2xl`, with ambient amber depth.
- **Imagery:** 4:5 or 1:1 ratio, full-bleed top with softened bottom internal radius, capturing pastry glaze, dusted sugars, and flaky layers.
- **Floating Badges:** Top-left rounded pill chips for "Fresh Out of the Oven", "Small Batch", or "Weekend Special".
- **Action Strip:** Base of the card houses localized flavor tasting notes, weight/servings, and an instant "Add to Box" or "Chat to Customize" pill button.

### Badges & Dietary Chips
- **Aesthetic:** Pill-shaped, delicate padding (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal), typeset in `label-sm`.
- **Palette Variants:**
  - *Fresh Batch:* Warm Caramel (`#C87D38`) text on Cream (`#FFFDF9`) base.
  - *Dietary (Gluten-Free, Nut-Free):* Chocolate (`#3D2314`) text on Biscuit Beige (`#F5EDE0`).
  - *Chef's Pick / Signature:* Chocolate text on Pastel Blush Pink (`#F7D6D0`).

### Form Inputs & Order Customizer
- **Text & Selection Fields:** Generous touch targets (48px minimum height), `rounded-xl` or full-pill inputs with `#F5EDE0` fill and borderless resting states. Focused states invoke a 1.5px caramel gold halo (`#C87D38`) without sharp outline breaks.
- **Flavour & Portion Radio Pills:** Segmented pill selectors that transition with smooth sliding cream surfaces upon selection.

### Interactive Micro-States
- Hover triggers produce subtle warmth: buttons gain a soft caramel under-glow, product cards lift seamlessly, and photo tags offer a gentle scale transform (1.02x) with smooth bezier curves.
