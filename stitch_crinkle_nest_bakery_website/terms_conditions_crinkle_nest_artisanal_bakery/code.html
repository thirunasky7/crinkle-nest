01 The Hearth Philosophy
bakery_dining

Fresh Batch Production & Artisanal Character

Every confection, sourdough loaf, and crinkle cookie bearing the Crinkle Nest seal is handcrafted in small batches inside our hearth boutique. We rigorously avoid commercial dough improvers, chemical leavening shortcuts, and synthetic food stabilizers.

auto_awesome Artisan Variations Notice

Because we rely on stone-ground heirloom flours and natural butter lamination, slight visual variations in the crinkle fracture patterns of our chocolate cookies, the caramelized blistering of sourdough crusts, or minor variations in ganache drape height are natural hallmarks of human craftsmanship. They do not constitute defects.

  • check_circle All sourdough undergoes an authentic 24- to 36-hour cold autolyse and bulk fermentation.
  • check_circle Daily inventory is governed strictly by the baking sheet limits of our stone-deck oven.
02 Transactions & Securing Batches
payments

Order Confirmation & Payment Terms

To honor the time-sensitive nature of baking schedules, orders are confirmed strictly upon 100% advance settlement via verified UPI, integrated digital payment gateways, or our direct WhatsApp conciergerie order ledger.

Standard Pastry & Loaf Boxes

Same-day reservations close at 11:00 AM daily or when the daily flour allocation has completed its final rise.

Celebration & Botanique Cakes

Require minimum 48 hours notice. Custom tiered creations require 5 days advance consultation and deposit.

In cases where a bank processing error fails to log against our WhatsApp confirmation window, our concierge will issue an immediate balance receipt or prompt release of holding authorizations within 24 business hours.

03 Logistics & Handover
electric_moped

Hearth Dispatch & Bicycle Delivery Protocol

We ship fragile, temperature-sensitive culinary goods across Bangalore urban limits through dedicated four-point balanced bicycle and gentle electric courier partners.

alarm_on Immediate Handover Policy

Because our brioche rolls, flaky croissants, and choux crèmes are vulnerable to ambient tropical heat, the recipient or a nominated party must be present at the pin location during the estimated 45-minute dispatch bracket. If delivery fails because the recipient cannot be contacted after 3 attempts, the courier is instructed to return the package to the hearth. Re-dispatch fees will apply.

layers All deliveries travel cushioned in thermal crumb envelopes and structured upright caddies to preserve delicate meringue peaks.

05 Health & Ingredients
nutrition

Allergens & Dietary Notice (Eggless & Nut Handling)

We operate an open, integrated bakery workshop where wheat flour dust, tree nuts (pistachios, valencia almonds, hazelnuts), and dairy are actively present.

egg_alt Eggless Kitchen Zone

Eggless orders are whipped using isolated silicone bowls, designated mixers, and separate deck racks.

grain Gluten Notice

Our "Gluten-Friendly" items are prepared with non-gluten flours, but our kitchen is NOT certified Celiac-safe.

spa Nut Isolation

Severe nut allergy sufferers are advised to consult our head pastry chef prior to placing an order.
